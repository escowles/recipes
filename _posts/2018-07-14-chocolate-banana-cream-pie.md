## chocolate cream filling:
  
5 oz. semi-sweet chocolate, chopped  
1/2 cup heavy cream or half-and-half  
* put chocolate in a heat-safe bowl
* heat cream/half-and-half to just boiling, pour over chocolate, and rest 2 minutes
* stir until chocolate is melted, smooth, and glossy (it will get grainy first)
* if using half-and-half, return to the pot and heat until it gets glossy
* rest 10 minutes

1/2 cup heavy cream
* beat until soft peaks form, then fold into chocolate
* pour into prepared pie crust, then chill 2 hours


## custard filling:
  
5 tbsp. sugar  
1 1/2 tbsp. cornstarch  
1/4 tsp. salt  
* mix in a small saucepan

3 egg yolks  
1 1/4 cup (10 oz.) half and half  
* add yolks and stir, then add half-and-half a little a time, stirring to incorporate
  and breaking up any lumps
* heat over medium heat while stirring, until thickened and bubbling (it's fine if
  it gets lumpy)

1 tbsp. butter  
1 tsp. vanilla  
* remove pot from heat and stir in butter and vanilla
* whisk until smooth, then cover with plastic wrap, pressing against the custard
  surface to prevent a skin forming
* rest at room temperature for 1 hour, then chill 1 hour


## assembly:

* prepare an [oreo crust]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2021-07-12-oreo-pie-crust %})
  or chocolate pie crust (see below) or [pastry crust]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2010-11-04-pie-crust %})
* fill crust with chocolate cream filling and chill as direct
* top with banana slices and add cooled custard on top
* decorate with more bananas then chill for 2 hours
* before serving, top each slice with [whipped cream]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2018-07-14-whipped-cream %}) and sliced bananas


## chocolate pie crust:
  
1 1/4 cup flour  
1/4 tsp. salt  
1/4 tsp. baking powder  
2 tbsp. cocoa powder  
1 tbsp. sugar  
8 tbsp. (1 stick) cold butter, cubed  
4-5 tbsp. cold water  
* add dry ingredients to food processor, and pulse to combine
* add butter and pulse until the texture is a coarse meal
* slowly add water, pulsing after each addition, until the dough clumps together and
  forms a ball
* roll crust out on a floured surface, to fit a 9" pie pan
* press crust into pan, trim edges, and poke holes in the bottom of the crust with
  a fork
* bake at 425°F for 10 minutes, then cool to room temperature

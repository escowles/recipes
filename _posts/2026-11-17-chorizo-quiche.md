1 [pie crust]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2010-11-04-pie-crust %})
* line pie dish with crust

8-9 oz. chorizo  
1/4 onion, diced  
1 poblano pepper, diced  
1/2 cup frozen corn  
1/2 cup black beans, rinsed and drained  
4 oz. shredded gruyere or cheddar  
* remove chorizo from casings and fry for 5 minutes, breaking up into small chunks
* add vegetables and cook until onion is soft, about 5 minutes
* sprinkle half the cheese in pie crust, spread chorizo/vegetables over it, and top with remaining
  cheese

3 eggs  
2/3 cup heavy cream  
2/3 cup whole milk  
3/4 tsp. salt  
1/2 tsp. black pepper  
1/4 tsp. chipotle powder  
* whisk in a medium bowl and pour into pie dish
* bake at 425°F for 15 minutes, then reduce heat to 300°F for 30 minutes
* remove from oven and rest 10 minutes before serving

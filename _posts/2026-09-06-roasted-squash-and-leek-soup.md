12 oz. butternut squash, peeled and cubed  
2 leeks, washed and thinly sliced  
1 tbsp. olive oil  
salt and pepper, to taste  
* toss squash and leek slices with oil and spread in a single layer on a baking sheet
* season with salt and pepper
* bake at 375°F for 25 minutes
* (optional) reserve some of the browned leeks for garnish

1 tbsp. olive oil  
2 cloves garlic, sliced  
4 cups vegetable broth  
1/4 cup cream  
* heat oil in a dutch oven and sauté garlic as the pan heats up
* add squash and leeks and sauté for 1 minute
* add broth and bring to a simmer for 10 minutes
* stir in cream and purée the soup with a stick blender until smooth
* taste and adjust salt and pepper, as needed
* garnish with reserved leeks

2 1/4 cups (200g) oats or oat flour  
3 tbsp. (41g) brown sugar  
2 tbsp. cornstarch  
1 tbsp. baking powder  
1/2 tsp. salt  
1/2 tsp. cinnamon  
* grind oats into flour in a blender or food processor
* combine in a large mixing bowl

2-3 bananas  
3 eggs  
1 cup milk  
1/2 cup butter, melted  
2 tsp. vanilla  
* mash bananas, then whisk in eggs and other ingredients
* pour into dry ingredients and stir until just combined (lumps are ok)
* rest 15 minutes, then stir the batter once more (it should be thick)
* make waffles with batter and keep warm in a 200°F oven until ready to serve

jalapeños  
neutral oil  
salt  
* pull stems off or trim with a knife
* toss with neutral oil and sprinkle with salt
* adjust oven rack to the highest setting and bake peppers at 450°F until they start to blacken,
  about 5 minutes
* turn peppers and roast other side until they start to blacken, about 5 more minutes
* remove peppers to a bowl, cover and rest 15 minutes to allow peppers to cool and skins loosen
* remove skins, slice in half lengthwise (remove seeds if desired)
* use fresh, refrigerate up to a couple of weeks, or freeze for long-term storage

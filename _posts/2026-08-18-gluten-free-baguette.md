## dough

1 cup plus 2 tbsp. (135g) king arthur gf bread flour  
5.5 oz. (156g) water, tepid  
1/2 tsp. (3g) table salt  
1 tsp. (4g) sugar  
1/4 teaspoon (1g) instant yeast  
* mix dough with a spatula
* transfer to a greased bowl, cover and rest 15 minutes
* every 15 minutes, perform a bowl fold (pull dough up from one side and press down into the middle
  of the bowl, 8-12 times)
* after 60 minutes (4 folds), cover and rest for 60 minutes

## preshape

* turn dough out onto a lightly floured surface and pat into a 6" square
* book-fold one direction, then fold in half three times the other direction to make a tube
* roll gently and press into a 6" tube
* cover and rest for 15 minutes

## shaping
* roll the tube into a 14" log, starting in the middle then out to each end
* taper each end to a point
* place the log in a flour-dusted couche, cover, and let rise for 60-75 minutes

## baking
* heat oven to 475°F with a cast iron pan on the lowest rack or bottom of the oven and a baking
  steel or baking sheet on the middle rack
* transfer proofed baguettes onto a piece of parchment, seam-side down
* cut 3 or 5 long cuts in the top of the baguette
* transfer the baguette to the oven (parchment and all)
* carefully pour 1 cup of water into the cast iron pan and quickly shut the door
* bake 24-28 minutes, until deep golden brown
* prop oven door open and cool completely on the rack for the crispiest crust, or remove to a
  cooling rack

source: <https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/easy-gluten-free-baguettes-recipe>

and the crust wound up being white and not very crunchy. but the bread was very flavorful and the
texture was dense. overall, probably the best GF bread i have had.

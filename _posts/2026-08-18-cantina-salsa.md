1 lb. tomatoes  
1/2 onion  
2 jalapeños  
1 clove garlic, sliced  
small bunch cilantro  
1 tsp. salt  
juice from 1 lime  
* roughly chop tomatoes and roast with jalapeños (see [roasted jalapeños]({{ site.baseurl }}{% post_url 2026-08-18-roasted-jalapeños %}))  
* for a milder salsa, remove the seeds and ribs from the jalapenos, otherwise chop them up
* add all ingredients to blender and pulse until desired consistency is reached
* chill for at least 1 hour to let flavors meld

source: <https://mexicanfoodjournal.com/restaurant-salsa/>

6 lbs. tomatoes, halved  
5-10 jalapeño peppers, halved and seeded  
1 bell pepper, quartered and seeded (optional)  
1 large onion, peeled and quartered  
10 cloves of garlic, peeled  
* bake vegetables at 375°F, cut side up, until they begin to soften, about 25 minutes
* broil for 2-5 minutes until they begin to char
* remove from oven and let cool slightly before processing, reserving pan juices

1 bunch cilantro, lower stems removed  
1 tbsp. salt  
* add to food processor or blender with roasted vegetables and pulse until chunky but not
  puréed, adding pan juices as needed
* adjust salt

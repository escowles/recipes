1 1/2 lbs. chicken thighs  
1 tsp. salt  
1 1/2 lbs. cherry tomatoes  
1" ginger, minced or grated  
3 tbsp. gochujang paste  
2 tbps. soy sauce  
1 tbsp. sesame oil  
1 tbsp. honey  
* combine in slow cooker and stir to combine
* cook on low heat for 6 hours, until chicken is tender and most of the tomatoes have burst
* if the sauce is too thin, remove liquid to a saucepan and boil to reduce it

3 scallions, sliced  
juice from 1 lime  
* stir in lime juice and scallions
* serve with rice or noodles

1 lb. dry pinto beans  
3 tbsp. kosher salt  
3 quarts water  
* sort beans, rinse well, and soak 8 hours (or overnight) in salted water
* drain and rinse beans immediately before cooking

8-10 cups water  
3/4 onion, diced  
1 tbsp. lard  
1 clove garlic, sliced  
1 tsp. epazote  
* add rinsed beans to a large pot and cover with water
* bring to a boil and skim off foam
* reduce heat to a simmer, and add remaining ingredients
* cook, loosely covered, until beans are very tender, about 90 minutes — add hot
  water as needed to keep the water level above the beans

1 tsp. salt
* uncover beans, add salt, and continue simmering until broth thickens, about 15
  minutes
* beans at this stage can be frozen with the broth, which should be about 4 cups of
  beans and 1 cup of liquid

1 tbsp. lard  
2 tbsp. diced white onion  
1 cup cooked pinto beans  
1/4 cup broth  
salt, to taste  
* heat lard in a saucepan and sauté the onion until softened
* add beans with a slotted spoon and sauté for 1 minute before mashing
* add broth and simmer until desired thickness, adding more broth and/or water as
  needed to adjust consistency
* season with salt to taste

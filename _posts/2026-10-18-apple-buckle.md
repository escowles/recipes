3/4 cup butter (1 1/2 sticks)  
* melt in an 8x8" baking dish

1 1/2 cups (192g) flour  
1 cup (200g) sugar  
2 tsp. kosher salt  
3/4 tsp. baking powder  
1 1/2 tsp. cinnamon
* whisk dry ingredients together in a mixing bowl

1 1/2 cup (360ml) milk  
1 1/2 tsp. vanilla extract
* add to dry ingredients and mix to make a batter
* pour in butter from baking dish and mix well
* pour batter back into pan

3-4 large apples  
* peel, slice thinly, and arrange atop the batter
* bake at 375°F for 75 minutes, until batter is set

## curry paste

1/2 tsp. coriander seeds  
1/2 tsp. cumin seeds  
1/4 cup peanut butter  
3 tbsp. red curry paste  
* dry-fry coriander and cumin seeds until fragrant, about 1 minute
* remove from pan, let cool, and grind with mortar and pestle
* mix with peanut butter and curry paste, and set aside


## chicken

1 lb. chicken breast or thighs  
2 tbsp. tamari  
* thinly slice chicken, toss with tamari, and set aside


## curry

13.5 oz. can coconut milk  
1 tsp. lime zest  
1 1/2 tsp. brown sugar  
1 red bell pepper, sliced  
1/4 onion, sliced and cut into thirds  
bamboo shoots or other veggies, to taste  
* scoop 1/4 cup of cream from coconut milk, and add to hot pan, stirring until
  thickened, about 30 seconds
* add curry paste, reduce heat to medium, and cook until the sauce breaks, 2-3
  minutes (adding a splash of coconut milk if it starts sticking)
* add lime zest, sugar, and veggies, and continue cooking while stirring until the
  veggies are softened
* add coconut milk and simmer until thickened, 4-8 minutes
* add chicken and simmer until chicken is thoroughly cooked, about 5 minutes
* serve with rice

8 oz. shredded mexican cheese blend  
8 oz. cheddar, shredded  
1 tbsp. pickled jalapeños, chopped  
1/4 onion, minced  
2 cloves garlic, minced  
10 oz. canned tomatoes with chiles  
* toss all ingredients in a mixing bowl and pour into a loaf pan
* smoke until cheese is melted, about 15 minutes
* stir and serve with chips

source: common recipe on Reddit (e.g, <https://www.reddit.com/r/smoking/comments/13vihdx/finally_did_the_infamous_reddit_smoked_queso/>)

## spice mix

2 tsp. salt  
2 tsp. five spice powder  
1 tsp. black pepper  
1 tsp. white pepper  
1 tsp. paprika  
1 tsp. garlic powder  
1 tsp. sugar  
1/4 tsp. red pepper flakes  


## assembly

2 tbsp. olive oil  
1 onion, chopped  
1 1/2 lbs. roasting veggies (potatoes, carrots, butternut squash, etc.), cubed  
1 tbsp. spice mix  
* toss veggies with oil, then sprinkle with spice mix and mix well
* transfer to baking dish and bake at 400°F for 20 minutes

1 1/2 lbs. chicken pieces  
remaining spice mix  
* remove baking dish from the oven and stir veggies
* toss chicken with remaining spice mix and add on top of the veggies
* continue baking until chicken is done (165°F internal temp.)
* check veggies and continue roasting as needed until tender

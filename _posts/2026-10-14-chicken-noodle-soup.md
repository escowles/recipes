2 tbsp. butter  
1/2 onion, diced  
12 baby carrots, sliced  
1/2 tsp. thyme  
1/2 tsp. rosemary  
1/2 tsp. pepper  
1/4 tsp. sage  
1/4 tsp. turmeric  
* melt butter in dutch oven and sauté veggies until softened and the bottom of the
  pot starts browning
* add spices and mix well

1 tbsp. olive oil  
1 1/3 lbs. chicken thighs  
1 qt. chicken stock  
* cut chicken into 1" cubes
* push veggies aside and add olive oil to bottom of pan
* sear chicken pieces and stir fry until browned
* add stock, bring to a boil, then simmer for 20 minutes
* remove chicken, let cool enough to handle (about 15 minutes) and shred
* return to to pot and continue simmering

9 oz. pasta (gluten-free pasta ok)  
* add to soup and cook according to package directions, testing to confirm tenderness
* remove from heat and rest 10 minutes

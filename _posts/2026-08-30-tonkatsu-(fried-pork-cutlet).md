## sauce

2 1/2 tbsp. ketchup  
2 tbsp. worcestershire sauce  
1 tbsp. sugar  
1 1/2 tsp. oyster sauce or hoisin sauce  
* whisk together in a small bowl and set aside


## tonkatsu

boneless pork loins or chops  
salt and pepper, to taste  
1/4 cup flour  
* get 1/4" chops if you can, otherwise pound pork cutlets to 1/4" thickness
* season with salt and pepper and dredge in flour

2 eggs  
1 tbsp. neutral oil  
1 cup panko  
* beat eggs and oil in a small bowl
* dip each cutlet in the mixture and then in panko, pressing down to coat well

oil for frying (3/4 cup was perfect for a 10" frying pan)
* heat oil to 350°F and fry cutlets in batches for 1 minute on each side, then remove
  to a paper-towel-lined plate to rest about 4 minutes while frying other cutlets
* fry each cutlet again until golden brown, about 30 seconds on each side, then
  remove to wire rack over a baking sheet in a 200°F oven
* serve with sauce and rice

250g smooth natural peanut butter  
42g honey  
100g brown sugar  
1 egg  
1 tsp. vanilla  
salt  
* mix together until smooth and thickened
* portion into 30g balls
* cross-hatch with a fork and sprinkle with salt
* bake at 350°F for 10 minutes, until edges begin to darken
* let cool on the baking sheet

## sauce

1 tbsp. neutral oil  
1 small onion  
1 clove garlic  
* roughly chop the onion and garlic if you plan to purée the sauce, otherwise, mince them
* heat oil in a saucepan and sauté onion and garlic until softened and starting to brown
  in places, about 10 minutes

1 cup (272g) ketchup  
1/4 cup water  
2 tbsp. apple cider vinegar  
1 tbsp. brown sugar  
2 tsp. curry powder  
1/2 tsp. paprika  
* add to pan and mix well
* simmer for 10-15 minutes, stirring frequently to prevent spattering

1/4 tsp. garam masala  
1/4 tsp. cayenne or chipotle powder (optional)  
* add to sauce and stir well, simmering for 1 additional minute
* purée the sauce for a smoother consistency

## currywurst

* grill or pan-fry bratwurst, slice into bite-sized rounds, and top with sauce
* serve with suace and french fries or roasted potatoes

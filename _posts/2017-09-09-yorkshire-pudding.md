1 1/2 cups / 200g flour  
3/4 tsp. salt  
3 eggs  
1 1/2 cups / 355ml milk, at room temperature  
3 tbsp. beef drippings or the fat of your choice  

* mix flour and salt
* whisk eggs and milk and add to dry ingredients in batches, mixing to get a smooth batter
* rest batter at least 30 minutes
* add 1 tsp. of fat to each well of a muffin pan and bake at 450°F for 5 minutes, until fat is
  melted
* quickly fill each well 1/3 full of batter and bake 20 mins without opening the oven

source: hobbit cookbook

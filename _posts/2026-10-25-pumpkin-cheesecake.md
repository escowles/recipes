24 oz. (680g) cream cheese, room temperature  
1 1/2 cups (320g) brown sugar  
* mix until smooth, about 5 minutes, scraping down bowl to make sure no lumps of
  cream cheese remain

15 oz. (425g) pumpkin purée  
4 eggs  
1/4 cup (57g) sour cream  
2 tbsp. flour  
1 tsp. cinnamon  
1/2 tsp. cloves  
1/2 tsp. allspice  
1/4 tsp. nutmeg  
1/4 tsp. ginger  
1/4 tsp. salt  
1 tbsp. vanilla  
* add to mixer and stir until combined
* prepare an oreo or graham cracker crust in a springform pan and line edges with
  parchment
* pour batter into the crust and drop onto counter several times to settle thoroughly
* add a pan of hot water to the oven and bake at 325°F until the edges are set and
  the center 3" is still wobbly, about 50-60 minutes
* open the oven door and prop slightly open while cooling in the oven for 30 minutes
* then continue cooling on a wire rack for 2 hours
* wrap in plastic and chill at least 4 hours before serving

source: <https://natashaskitchen.com/pumpkin-cheesecake-recipe/>

1/4 cup water  
3 tbsp. honey  
3/4 tsp. black pepper  
1/2 tsp. kosher salt  
* mix together in a small bowl and set aside

2 tbsp. flour  
1 1/2 tsp. turmeric  
1 tsp. kosher salt  
1 lb. chicken, cubed  
* mix flour, turmeric, and salt together in a medium bowl
* add chicken and toss to coat

1 tbsp. neutral oil  
12 oz. asparagus, thinly sliced on an angle  
1 tsp. rice vinegar  
juice from 1 lime  
* heat oil in a skillet or dutch oven, add chicken and sauté 2-3 minutes per side, until turmeric is
  fragrant and chicken is browned
* add asparagus and stir fry until crisp-tender, about 2 minutes
* add honey mixture and cook until chicken is cooked through and sauce thickens, about 3 minutes
* remove from heat and stir in vinegar and lime juice

3 tbsp. olive oil  
1 onion, diced  
salt and pepper, to taste  
* heat oil in a pan and sauté onion until softened, about 5 minutes
* season with salt and pepper

1 1/2 cups arborio rice  
* add to onion and continue cooking until onion begins to brown, about 2 minutes

1/4 tsp. red pepper flakes  
2 cloves garlic, minced  
1/2 cup white wine  
2 cups tomatoes, diced  
* add to pan and cook until most of the liquid has evaporated, about 5 minutes

3 cups boiling water or vegetable/chicken stock  
* add one ladle at a time and stir rice every minute or so as it simmers down
* check rice and add additional hot water as needed to continue simmering until
  rice is tender

1/2 cup (50g) pecorino or parmesan, grated  
2 tbsp. olive oil  
4 medium tomatoes, sliced  
salt and pepper, to taste  
chopped herbs, for garnish  
* turn off heat and stir in cheese and oil
* serve with sliced tomatoes seasoned with salt and pepper, and herbs

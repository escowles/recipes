## salmon and asparagus

4 salmon fillets  
2 bunches asparagus  
salt and pepper, to taste  
2 tbsp. olive oil  
juice from 1 lemon  
zest from 1/2 lemon  
1 dill sprig, chopped  
* arrange salmon on one side of a baking sheet
* trim asparagus and arrange in a single layer on the other side
* season with salt and pepper and drizzle with oil and lemon juice
* top salmon with lemon zest and dill
* bake at 400°F until salmon reaches desired level of doneness (125°F for medium-rare
  up to 140°F for medium-well), about 12-15 minutes

## sauce

1/2 cup mayonnaise  
1/2 cup plain yogurt or sour cream  
2 tbsp. dijon mustard  
4 dill sprigs, chopped  
zest and juice from 1 lemon  
1/4 tsp. salt
1/4 tsp. pepper
* combine all ingredients in a small bowl and mix well

note: can be served hot, or chilled and served cool or at room temperature.

source: What to Cook When You Don't Feel Like Cooking, p. 40.

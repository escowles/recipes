## saffron stock

2 1/2 cups chicken stock  
1/2 tsp. saffron threads  
* sprinkle saffron into warm stock and set aside


## meat

1 tbsp. olive oil  
5 oz. chicken breast, cubed  
5 oz. chorizo, sliced  
* heat oil in a large pan and sauté chicken and chorizo until browned
* remove from pan and cover to keep warm


## main

1/2 lb. green beans  
1/2 red bell pepper, diced  
1/2 spanish onion, diced  
2 cloves garlic, minced  
1 tsp. paprika  
1 tomato, chopped  
* add green beans, bell pepper, and onion to pan and sauté 3 minutes
* add garlic and sauté 30 seconds
* add paprika and tomatoes and sauté 5 minutes
* add saffron stock and meats to the pan and dip a toothpick in the liquid to mark the level
* add 4 cups of water and simmer on high for 10 minutes, then reduce heat to low and simmer for an
  additional 15-20 minutes, until the water level returns to the mark on the toothpick

1 cups short-grain rice
8 medium shrimp  
8 sea scallops  
fresh parsley, chopped
* add rice, shrimp, scallops, and parsley to the pan and stir to evenly combine
* simmer 10 minutes without stirring, then reduce heat to a low simmer until liquid is absorbed
* increase heat for 1-2 minutes to crisp the bottom at the end, being careful not to scorch the rice
* remove from heat and cover with a kitchen towel for 5 minutes before serving

## dal

1 cup (200g) split yellow lentils (moong dal)  
* soak lentils in water for 15 minutes, then drain

1 to 2 tbsp. neutral oil  
1 medium onion, diced  
* heat oil until shimmering, then sauté onions until softened (5-7 minutes)
* (optional) cook an additional 5-10 minutes until well-browned for added flavor

4 cloves garlic, chopped  
1" fresh ginger, grated  
1 serrano pepper, finely chopped  
* add to onions and stir frequently for 60-90 seconds until fragrant

1 tsp. Madras curry powder  
1 tsp. garam masala  
1/4 tsp. coriander  
1/4 tsp. turmeric  
* add to onions and stir constantly for 30 seconds

drained lentils  
2 cups water  
salt and pepper, to taste  
* add and bring to a boil, then reduce heat and loosely cover and simmer until lentils are
  tender, about 30 minutes, adding additional water as needed to maintain consistency

14.5 oz. canned tomatoes  
1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped  
* add tomatoes and cook until broken down, about 4-5 minutes (more time will be needed if
  substituting fresh, which can be puréed to speed things up)
* add cilantro, adjust seasoning, and serve with tadka

## tadka

2 tbsp. neutral oil
1 tsp. mustard seeds
1/2 tsp. cumin seeds
pinch asafetida
10 fresh curry leaves
1-2 dried red chilies
* heat oil in smallest pan
* add mustard seeds and swirl pan until they start to pop
* add cumin seeds, then remaining ingredients, swirling for 15-30 seconds until curry leaves start to shrivel
* remove from heat and pour immediately over dal

source: <https://rainbowplantlife.com/restaurant-style-vegan-dal-tadka/>

1 tbsp. butter  
18 oz. (500g) banana bread, cubed  
* butter an 8x8" baking dish and add cubed banana bread to it

2 bananas  
4 eggs  
1/3 cup (57g) brown sugar  
1 1/2 cups (355ml) milk  
1 1/2 cups (355ml) cream  
1 tbsp. vanilla  
1 tsp. salt  
* mash bananas, then mix in remaining ingredients
* pour over banana bread cubes and rest at least 30 minutes (up to 4 hours)
* bake at 350°F for 50-60 minutes, until browned and mostly set (some jiggle is fine) and internal
  temperature reaches 175°F

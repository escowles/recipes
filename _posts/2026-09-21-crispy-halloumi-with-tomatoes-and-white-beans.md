2 tbsp. olive oil  
1 lb. cherry or grape tomatoes, halved lengthwise or other tomatoes roughly chopped  
2 cloves garlic, minced  
1 tsp. honey  
1 tsp. parsley  
1 tsp. thyme  
salt and pepper, to taste  
* in a large, ovenproof pan, sauté all ingredients until tomatoes soften and release
  their juices, about 10 minutes

15 oz. can cannellini beans, butter beans or navy beans, drained  
* stir in beans and cook until heated through, about 3 minutes.

8 oz. block halloumi  
* slice into 1/4" thick slices and arrange on top of tomato mixture
* broil on high heat until crispy and golden, about 5 minutes

2 tbsp. olive oil  
1 tsp. honey  
juice from 1/2 lime  
* drizzle with olive oil and honey, then squeeze the lime over the pan

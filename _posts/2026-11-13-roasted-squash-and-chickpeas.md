1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed  
* spread on paper towels and let dry

1 1/4 lbs. squash, cubed  
1 tbsp. olive oil  
1 tsp. salt  
1 tsp. thyme  
1 tsp. garam masala  
1/2 tsp. cumin  
pinch red pepper flakes  
* toss to combine and roast at 425°F for 20 minutes

1/2 onion, thinly sliced  
* toss with chickpeas and add to the squash
* continue roasting at 425°F for 30 minutes, tossing halfway through

1 tsp. apple cider vinegar  
1 tbsp. hot honey  
* toss with squash/chickpeas, taste, and adjust seasoning

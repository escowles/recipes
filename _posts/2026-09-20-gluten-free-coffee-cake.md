3/4 cup sugar  
1/2 cup butter, softened  
2 eggs  
2/3 cup yogurt  
1/4 cup milk  
* cream sugar and butter, then blend in eggs, yogurt, and milk

1 1/2 gluten-free flour  
3/4 cup almond flour  
2 tsp. baking powder  
3/4 tsp. salt  
* add to wet ingredients and mix until incorporated

1 1/2 tbsp. brown sugar  
1 1/2 tsp. cinnamon  
* spread half the batter in a greased 8x8" baking pan, then sprinkle with brown sugar
  and cinnamon
* top with remaining batter

1/2 cup gluten-free flour  
1/4 cup butter, softened  
1/4 cup brown sugar  
2 tsp. cinnamon  
1/4 tsp. salt  
* mix well and sprinkle over batter
* bake at 350°F for 38-42 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean
* cool in pan for 15 minutes

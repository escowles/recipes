227g Bob's Red Mill gluten-free pie crust mix (1/2 package)  
4 tbsp. (56g, 1/2 stick) butter, cubed  
4 tbsp. (48g) shortening  
5 tbsp. water
* mix butteer and shortening into pie crust mix with a pastry blender or food processor
* add water and mix well to form a dough
* shape into a disc, wrap in plastic wrap, and chill 30 minutes to let the dough hydrate
* roll out between sheets of baking parchment or waxed paper to desired pie dish size

source: Bob's Red Mill gluten-free pie crust mix package

---

11/27/2026: After some problems with using GF flour with my standard pie crust recipe, I tried a few
different mixes recently. This was the best one, and worked well for thanksgiving pies.

2 roma tomatoes, cubed  
1/4 red onion, diced  
1 clove garlic, minced  
1/2 serrano pepper, minced  
juice from 1 limes  
1 tsp. kosher salt  
1 tsp. olive oil  
1/4 bunch cilantro, chopped  
* combine all ingredients, being careful not to add too much liquid
* taste and adjust salt, lime juice, etc. as needed
* cover and chill at least 10 minutes, up to 3 hours

source: <https://cooking.nytimes.com/recipes/1026323-salpicon-de-pescado-spicy-citrus-marinated-fish>

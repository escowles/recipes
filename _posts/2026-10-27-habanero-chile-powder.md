40 habenero chiles  
* wearing gloves, halve habaneros, remove seed cores, and spread in a single layer on a baking sheet
* bake at lowest oven setting until peppers are dried and break instead of bending, 4-8 hours
* let cool to room temperature before storing in a glass jar
* wearing gloves and a mask, grind with a mortar and pestle to make chile powder

yield: 30g of chile powder

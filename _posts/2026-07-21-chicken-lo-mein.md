## noodles:

8 oz. rice noodles  
* prepare according to shortest time on package directions
* drain and set aside

## chicken:

2 tsp. cornstarch  
2 tsp. water  
2 tsp. neutral oil  
1 tsp. oyster sauce  
1/2 tsp. baking soda  
8 oz. chicken thighs, sliced into thin strips
* dissolve cornstarch in water, then add remaining ingredients and mix to coat the chicken

## sauce:

2 tbsp. hot water  
1/4 tsp. salt  
1/4 tsp. sugar  
2 tbsp. tamari  
1 tsp. sesame oil  
1/4 tsp. white pepper  
* dissolve salt and sugar in hot water, then mix in remaining ingredients

## stir fry:

neutral oil, as needed  
2 cloves garlic, minced  
1" ginger, minced or grated  
4 cups shredded/julienned vegetables (cabbage, carrots, scallions, peppers, etc.)  
1 tbsp. dry cooking wine  
* sear chicken until browned, then remove from the pan and set aside
* sauté garlic and ginger for 10 seconds
* add shredded vegetables and stir fry for 1-2 minutes
* add the cooking wine around the edge of the pan
* add noodles, chicken, and sauce and toss to coat the noodles
* if noodles are too dry, add 1/4 cup water or stock to loosen them
* continue stir frying until the noodles are loose, heated, and coated in sauce — they should look glossy
